# Community Standards

arc-tree is a small open-source project. These are the norms we hold everyone to — contributors, maintainers, and anyone who opens an issue.

## The short version

Be professional and constructive. Treat everyone with the same respect you'd want in a work setting.

## What we expect

- Clear, specific feedback — "this breaks X because Y" rather than vague criticism
- Patience with newcomers; everyone was new once
- Disagreement about code or design is fine; personal attacks are not
- Credit others' work and ideas

## What we don't tolerate

- Insults, mockery, or dismissive comments directed at a person
- Unsolicited personal remarks unrelated to the code
- Repeated messaging after someone has asked you to stop
- Publishing someone's private information without permission

## Enforcement

If something feels wrong, email **kobecuppens@gmail.com**. Reports are kept private. The maintainer will review and respond within 7 days. Depending on severity, the outcome may be a note, a warning, or a permanent ban from the project.

We apply these standards to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other venue where arc-tree is the context.
